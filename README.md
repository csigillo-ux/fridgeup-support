## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I add a product?** Tap the green "+" button at the bottom of the screen and enter the product details manually or scan the barcode.

**How do I upgrade to PRO?** Tap the settings icon (top right) to access the PRO upgrade screen.

**What's included in PRO?**

- Multiple lists (Fridge, Freezer, Pantry)
- Shared fridge: create a group with the same list for everyone
- iOS Home Screen Widget
- 5 exclusive themes
- 5 alternative app icons

**What is free?** The free app includes unlimited products, barcode and date scanning, notifications, iCloud sync across your devices, and joining a shared fridge with an invite code.
